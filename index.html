This Privacy Policy explains how ("iam", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use 2048 World ("App").

By using 2048 World, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

2048 World stores certain game-related information locally on your device so that your progress and settings can be maintained.

This may include:

  • Game progress and saved game state
  • Scores and best scores
  • World Tour progress
  • Coins and in-game rewards
  • Tour Tokens
  • Game settings such as sound and haptic preferences
  • Selected game mode
  • Other gameplay-related preferences

This information is stored locally on your device using the application's local storage mechanisms.

2048 World does not currently require you to create an account or provide your name, phone number, password, or email address to play the game.

Advertising-Related Information

2048 World uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner advertisements and rewarded advertisements.

Google AdMob and its associated technologies may collect or process information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising information, diagnostic information, and information about ad interactions, depending on the applicable device, configuration, consent, and Google policies.

This information may be used for purposes including:

  • Providing advertisements
  • Measuring advertisement performance
  • Preventing fraud and abuse
  • Improving advertising services
  • Providing personalized or non-personalized advertising where applicable

Google's handling of information is governed by Google's applicable privacy policies.

2. How We Use Information

Information used by 2048 World is primarily used to:

  • Save and restore your game progress
  • Maintain your scores and achievements
  • Maintain your coins and Tour Tokens
  • Remember your game preferences and settings
  • Provide the World Tour experience
  • Display advertisements
  • Provide rewarded advertisements and associated in-game rewards
  • Improve the stability and functionality of the App

We do not use your locally stored game information to personally identify you.

Game-related information stored locally by the App remains on your device unless it is removed by you, the App, or the operating system.
